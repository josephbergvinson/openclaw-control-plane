# Company writing

Style guidance for substantial company-facing prose. `SOUL.md` sets the voice; `AGENTS.md` and `TOOLS.md` retain authority over sources, privacy, actions and verification. A requested draft is still a draft until its intended publication or delivery is authorized.

Lead with the answer, recommendation or thesis. Write for the actual audience and assume their domain competence. Develop the argument through mechanisms, evidence and consequences; use the subject's own vocabulary rather than internal operating or verification language. Risks and unresolved questions should qualify a useful recommendation, not displace it.

Preserve analytical depth and a distinctive voice. Longer sentences are useful when their clauses carry causality or conceptual pressure. First-person reflection can fit writing from inside the work. Parallel lists and coined terms earn their place by doing explanatory work. Keep hedges that express real uncertainty; remove empty intensifiers, motivational padding, moral performance and decorative slogans.

For technical and operational writing, introduce a concrete mechanism or failure early, scope claims to available evidence, and end on a useful decision or consequence. For philosophical essays, engage actual concepts and positions, build coherent paragraph-level arguments, and connect abstraction to material causality. Do not substitute a stepwise explainer or name-dropping for the argument. Governance proposals should make the mechanism, incentives, tradeoffs and implementation consequences clear.

Draft for argument and structure, then edit silently. Remove repetitive setup/payoff contrasts, abstract throat-clearing, aphoristic conclusions and announcements of the writing process where they add no substance. Preserve rhetorical force when shortening would flatten the meaning. These are judgment calls, not forbidden-word rules.

Use a restrained heading hierarchy and readable paragraph density. Put comparisons in tables only when useful. Keep private information, credentials, host paths, tool scaffolding and internal process notes out of company deliverables. Cite relevant sources near the claims they support and distinguish current facts from proposals. Return the requested prose, not an account of having written it.

Before delivery, inspect the rendered result when presentation matters and walk through one representative reader action using only the delivered content. The reader should find a concise recommendation, the needed material and a clear next step. For visual or craft work, show decisive examples beside the explanation of what to borrow or change. Apply the relevant existing brand guide, template or governing assets after establishing the applicable version. Keep detailed source notes, methodology and internal QA separate, with supporting evidence easy to reach.
