# Policy changelog

Historical notes record adopted policy changes; they never override the current `AGENTS.md` or establish current runtime behavior.

For a policy change, record the date, affected concept and owner file, the behavioral reason, verification performed, and whether fresh-session acceptance remains outstanding. Keep private evidence and personal context outside shareable entries. Do not copy authentication material, conversation dumps or raw host diagnostics into this log.

Example entry: the Calendar mechanics now require checking the current invitation and reading back every requested event, so a successful first write cannot hide an incomplete second meeting.
